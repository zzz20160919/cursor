# Step 3 weight-cap sensitivity at min N = 5

High and Low must each contain at least 5 contracts, the same rule as the main min-N row. A name's weight is its share of that day's High portfolio, or separately of that day's Low portfolio, after the usual within-category open-interest weights and the presort category weights. Weight above the cap is cash earning zero. The other names keep their original weights. Nothing is scaled back up to one.

The no-cap row matches `step3_min_n_sensitivity.csv`. Official Step 3 files and the min-N files were not rewritten. Paper Table IA.15 YESMV is Low +0.084, High −0.171, High−Low −0.255 (t = −5.81).
